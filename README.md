# Slime-Slider
Immerse yourself into Slime Slider, literally! This Match-3 game is all about controlled match-making, because YOU will controlling these little slimes and buddying them up with their friends, As these lil guys get sad, they begin to glow, that's when YOU take over! Possess their bodies to slip' n' slide across the tiles and help make loads of the slimes' days.

This game is playable on the web, on itch.io at __ (will add the link later)

How to play:
1. Click quickly on slimes when they start to glow
2. Move the glowing slime with WASD to swap positions with other slimes
3. Try to make a match with other slimes of the same colour
4. Click the next glowing slime after making a match or if the glow faded from your controlled slime
5. Try to meet the objective before the timer runs out!

Repeat this loop of sliding your slimes across the grid to make matches for each level.
